# Smart Sri Lankan Healthcare Management Platform - Scope

## Overview
The Smart Sri Lankan Healthcare Platform is an integrated healthcare management and predictive analytics suite designed for Sri Lankan healthcare providers, international patients, doctors, lab technicians, pharmacists, and hospital administrators.

## Core Features
1. **Public & Auth Services**: Registration, role-based login, international patient inquiries.
2. **Patient Management**: Appointment booking, lab results viewing, prescription tracking, billing history.
3. **Doctor Portal**: Daily schedule, OPD queue status, patient history, lab requests, e-prescribing, risk predictions.
4. **Admin Dashboard**: Real-time hospital metrics, staff & user management, pharmacy stock management, billing analytics.
5. **AI/ML Engine**: Appointment no-show prediction, health risk classification, feedback sentiment analysis, anomaly detection.
